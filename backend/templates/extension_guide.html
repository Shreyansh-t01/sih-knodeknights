Install GovBridge Chrome Recorder Extension

Record user actions on any website with smart noise filtering, resilient selector capture, and 1-click redirect to GovBridge builder.

3-Step Quick Installation (Unpacked Extension)

1

Open Chrome Extensions Manager

Open Google Chrome, navigate to chrome://extensions in your address bar, and switch on the Developer mode toggle in the top-right corner.

2

Click "Load unpacked"

Click the Load unpacked button and select the extension folder on your system:

c:\RPA Bots\extension
3

Start Recording & Trace Paths

Click the GovBridge icon in the Chrome toolbar, click Start Recording, perform your interactions on any target website, and when you click Stop & Send to GovBridge, it automatically redirects you to the visual builder with the full path traced!
